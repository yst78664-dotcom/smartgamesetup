    Step-by-Step Setup Guide: Nanoleaf Rhythm + Spotify

    Phase 1: Install and Position Your Nanoleaf Panels (15 minutes)

    1. Plan Your Gaming Layout

    • Behind monitor: Creates immersive backlighting effect
    • Side walls: Provides ambient gaming atmosphere
    • Above desk: Gives overhead accent lighting
    • Corner installation: Maximizes light distribution

    2. Mount Your Panels

    • Use included mounting strips on clean, dry walls
    • Start with base panel, add others in your desired pattern
    • Leave space for Rhythm module connection
    • Test pattern before final mounting

    3. Connect Power and Controller

    • Connect main controller to first panel
    • Plug in power adapter (ensure adequate wattage for your panel count)
    • Test basic on/off functionality

    Phase 2: Install Nanoleaf Rhythm Module (10 minutes)

    1. Physical Installation

    • Power off your Nanoleaf panels
    • Connect Rhythm module to any panel's expansion port
    • The module has a small microphone and audio input jack
    • Position microphone away from fan noise, towards your audio source

    2. Audio Connection Options

    Option A: Microphone-Only (Easiest)

    • Use built-in microphone to detect room audio
    • Works with headphones + speakers combination
    • Good for casual gaming

    Option B: Direct Audio Cable (Recommended)

    • Connect 3.5mm audio cable from PC audio output to Rhythm module
    • Provides cleanest signal with no background noise
    • Best for precise music synchronization

    Option C: Audio Splitter Setup (Best for Headset Users)

    • Use Y-splitter to send audio to both headset and Rhythm module
    • Maintains private audio while enabling light sync
    • Perfect for streaming or late-night gaming

    Phase 3: Configure Nanoleaf App for Gaming (15 minutes)

    1. Download and Set Up Nanoleaf App

    • Install from App Store/Google Play
    • Create account and connect to your panels
    • Ensure firmware is updated to latest version

    2. Enable Rhythm Mode

    • Open app → Select your panel setup
    • Tap "Rhythm" → Enable music visualization
    • Choose "Rhythm" as your active scene

    3. Adjust Gaming-Optimized Settings

    Sensitivity Settings:

    • High sensitivity (80-90%): For quiet gaming music, ambient tracks
    • Medium sensitivity (50-70%): For most gaming genres
    • Low sensitivity (20-40%): For intense metal, EDM, bass-heavy tracks

    Frequency Response:

    • Bass-focused: Perfect for electronic gaming music
    • Mid-range: Great for rock and alternative soundtracks
    • Treble-focused: Ideal for classical or orchestral gaming scores
    • Full spectrum: Best for varied gaming playlists

    Troubleshooting Common Issues

    "No Music Response" Problems

    Issue: Lights don't react to Spotify music
    Solutions:

    1. Check audio cable connection to Rhythm module
    2. Increase microphone sensitivity in app
    3. Verify Spotify is playing at adequate volume
    4. Test with high-bass music (easier to detect)
    5. Restart Nanoleaf panels and reconnect

    Delayed or Laggy Response

    Issue: Lights respond 1-2 seconds after music beats
    Solutions:

    1. Use direct audio cable instead of microphone
    2. Reduce WiFi interference (change router channel)
    3. Update Nanoleaf app and panel firmware
    4. Move panels closer to WiFi router
    5. Close other apps using significant bandwidth

    Oversensitive or Undersensitive Response

    Issue: Lights react to every tiny sound or don't react enough
    Solutions:

    1. Adjust sensitivity slider in Rhythm settings
    2. Change frequency response settings
    3. Test with different music genres
    4. Check for background noise affecting microphone
    5. Use audio cable for cleaner signal

    Colors Don't Match Music Mood

    Issue: Lights show wrong colors for music genre
    Solutions:

    1. Create custom scenes for specific playlists
    2. Adjust color palette in Rhythm settings
    3. Use scene scheduling for different gaming times
    4. Test with genre-appropriate frequency settings
    5. Create manual scenes as backup
