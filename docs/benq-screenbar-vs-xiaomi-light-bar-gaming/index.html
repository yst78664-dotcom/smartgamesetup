    Why Xiaomi's remote makes sense: Wireless remote can be placed anywhere on your desk. No cables, no clutter. Battery life lasts 6+ months with daily use. All controls accessible from one device.

    Which Monitor Light Bar Should You Buy?

    Choose BenQ ScreenBar Plus if:

    • You game 4+ hours daily and want premium eye comfort
    • Budget isn't your main concern ($100+ is acceptable)
    • You want set-and-forget lighting with auto-dimming
    • Build quality matters and you prefer premium materials
    • You have a high-end gaming setup and want matching quality accessories

    Choose Xiaomi Mi Light Bar if:

    • Budget is important and $39 fits better than $109
    • You game 1-3 hours daily and don't mind manual adjustments
    • You're trying monitor lighting for the first time and want low-risk entry
    • Remote control appeals more than desktop dial controls
    • You're building a budget gaming setup and need good value

    Final Verdict: Context Matters

    For serious gamers: BenQ ScreenBar Plus justifies its premium price with superior brightness, auto-dimming convenience, and long-term durability. The $70 extra cost pays dividends in daily comfort.

    For budget builders: Xiaomi Mi Light Bar delivers 80% of the benefit at 35% of the cost. It's an excellent introduction to monitor lighting without the premium price tag.

    The surprise winner? Xiaomi impresses with its value proposition. Unless auto-dimming and maximum brightness are must-haves, the Xiaomi provides exceptional bang for your buck.
