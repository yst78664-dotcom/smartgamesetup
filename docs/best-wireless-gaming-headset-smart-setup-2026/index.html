    Common Issues & Solutions

    Wireless Connectivity Problems

    Issue: Audio cutting out or latency
    Solution: Use 2.4GHz dongle, avoid interference from WiFi routers

    Battery Drain Too Fast

    Issue: Battery life shorter than expected
    Solution: Disable RGB lighting, enable auto-shutoff, update firmware

    Uncomfortable During Long Sessions

    Issue: Ear fatigue, headband pressure
    Solution: Adjust headband tension, try different ear pad materials

    Microphone Not Working

    Issue: Others can't hear you clearly
    Solution: Update drivers, adjust sidetone, clean mic boom
