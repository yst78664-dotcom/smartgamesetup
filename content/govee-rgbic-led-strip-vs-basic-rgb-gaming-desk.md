## What's the Difference? RGBIC vs RGB Explained

### Basic RGB: One Color, Whole Strip
**RGB strips** display **one solid color** across the entire length. Think of it like painting your desk lighting with a single color:

✅ **Pros:** Cheaper, cleaner look, less distracting  
✅ **Great for:** Solid ambient lighting, minimalist setups  
❌ **Cons:** No color zones, limited effects  

### RGBIC: Multiple Colors, Multiple Zones
**RGBIC strips** can display **different colors simultaneously** along the length. Each LED (or small group) can be independently controlled:

✅ **Pros:** Dynamic effects, color waves, zone control  
✅ **Great for:** Streaming, immersive gaming, eye-catching setups  
❌ **Cons:** More expensive, can be distracting during gameplay
