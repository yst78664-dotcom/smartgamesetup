## Image Quality: The Core Comparison

### 1080p60 Streaming Quality

**Razer Kiyo Pro:**
- **Sensor performance:** Sony STARVIS 1/2.8" delivers excellent low-light performance
- **Color science:** Vibrant, slightly saturated colors that work well for gaming content
- **Sharpness:** Good detail retention with minimal over-sharpening
- **Auto-exposure:** Generally stable but can struggle with rapid lighting changes
- **Field of view options:** Multiple FOV settings (103°/90°/80°/65°) for perfect framing

**Elgato Facecam MK.2:**
- **Sensor performance:** Sony STARVIS CMOS optimized specifically for streaming
- **Color science:** Superior color accuracy with more natural skin tones
- **Sharpness:** Excellent detail with professional-grade image processing
- **Auto-exposure:** Rock-solid exposure control, rarely hunts or overshoots
- **Fixed FOV:** 82° field of view works well for most streaming setups

**Winner:** Elgato Facecam MK.2. The streaming-optimized sensor and superior color processing deliver consistently better image quality.

### Low Light Performance

**Test conditions:** Typical gaming room at night with minimal ambient lighting (150-200 lux)

**Razer Kiyo Pro:**
- **Built-in lighting:** RGB ring light provides even facial illumination
- **Sensor performance:** Good low-light capabilities but relies on ring light for optimal results
- **Noise levels:** Moderate grain in very dim conditions without ring light
- **Color retention:** Colors remain fairly accurate with ring light assistance

**Elgato Facecam MK.2:**
- **Natural performance:** Excels in low light without requiring additional illumination
- **Sensor advantage:** Superior noise handling in dim conditions
- **Color consistency:** Maintains natural color balance even in challenging lighting
- **Professional results:** Delivers broadcast-quality image in low light

**Winner:** Elgato Facecam MK.2. While the Kiyo Pro's ring light helps, the Facecam MK.2's superior sensor performance means better results even without additional lighting.

### HDR Performance

**Razer Kiyo Pro:**
- **720p HDR:** Offers HDR recording at 720p60 maximum resolution
- **Dynamic range:** Good shadow and highlight detail with HDR enabled
- **Processing:** Natural-looking HDR without over-processing artifacts
- **Limitation:** HDR forces resolution down to 720p, losing detail for streaming

**Elgato Facecam MK.2:**
- **1080p HDR:** Maintains full 1080p resolution with HDR processing
- **Dynamic range:** Excellent shadow/highlight balance with professional-grade processing
- **Stream integration:** HDR processing optimized for streaming workflows
- **No compromises:** Full resolution HDR perfect for streaming platforms

**Winner:** Elgato Facecam MK.2. Full 1080p HDR vs 720p HDR is no contest for streaming quality.
