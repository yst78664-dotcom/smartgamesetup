## Troubleshooting Common Gaming Desk Charging Issues

### 🔧 Problem-Solving Guide

**Phone Won't Charge:**
- Remove thick cases (>5mm)
- Center device on charging pad
- Check for metal objects interfering
- Verify Qi compatibility

**Slow Charging Speeds:**
- Use included power adapter (not PC USB)
- Ensure phone case supports wireless charging
- Check for overheating (pause charging if needed)
- Update device software for charging optimizations

**Interference with Gaming Setup:**
- Keep charger 6+ inches from wireless gaming mice
- Avoid placing near 2.4GHz wireless receivers
- Test different desk positions if connectivity issues occur
