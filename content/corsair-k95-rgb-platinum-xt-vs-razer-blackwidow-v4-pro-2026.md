## Switch Performance: Different Strengths for Different Gamers

### Corsair K95 Switch Options

The K95 comes with **Cherry MX switches** — the gold standard for mechanical keyboards:

- **Cherry MX Speed (Linear):** 1.2mm actuation, 45g force — perfect for gaming
- **Cherry MX Brown (Tactile):** 2mm actuation, 45g force — great all-around choice

**Gaming performance:** Cherry MX Speed switches are **unmatched for competitive gaming** with their ultra-fast 1.2mm actuation point. You'll register keypresses significantly faster than standard switches.

### Razer BlackWidow V4 Pro Switch Options

The BlackWidow V4 Pro offers **Razer's proprietary switches** with hot-swap capability:

- **Razer Green (Clicky):** 1.9mm actuation, 50g force — tactile with audible click
- **Razer Yellow (Linear):** 1.2mm actuation, 45g force — smooth and quiet
- **Razer Orange (Tactile):** 1.9mm actuation, 45g force — tactile bump without click

**Gaming performance:** Razer Yellow switches match Cherry MX Speed for gaming, but the **hot-swappable design** lets you experiment with different switches without buying a new keyboard.

**Winner: Tie** — Both offer excellent gaming switches, but Razer wins on customization flexibility.

## RGB Lighting: Both Shine Bright

### Corsair K95 RGB Lighting

The K95's **19-zone LightEdge** lighting around the perimeter is stunning and unique. Combined with per-key RGB, it creates immersive lighting effects that sync with games, music, and system temps.

**iCUE integration** allows complex lighting profiles that sync across all Corsair peripherals in your setup.

### Razer BlackWidow V4 Pro RGB Lighting

**Razer Chroma RGB** with per-key lighting offers over 16.8 million colors. The lighting is vibrant and uniform across all keys, with excellent integration into the Razer ecosystem.

**Chroma integration** works with hundreds of games for immersive lighting effects that react to in-game events.

**Winner: Corsair K95** — The unique LightEdge perimeter lighting gives it a more distinctive look.
