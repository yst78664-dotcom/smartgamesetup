**Small desks (under 40" wide):** Choose 31-32" mats like the ITNRSIIET or BladeHawks
**Medium desks (40-50" wide):** Go with 35-40" options like the AIMSA
**Large desks (over 50" wide):** The Cacoy XXL at 48" will give you full coverage

### Choose LED-Only Mats (Most Options) if:
- You want maximum lighting mode variety (10-14 options)
- Budget is the primary concern ($25-45)
- You prefer simple plug-and-play operation
- Lighting effects are your main priority

### Choose Wireless Charging Mats (GIM) if:
- You regularly charge Qi-compatible devices
- Desk organization and cable reduction matter
- You're willing to pay extra for convenience features
- You want the latest tech integration

### Choose Themed Designs (AIMSA, Map Design) if:
- Your gaming room has a specific aesthetic theme
- You want your setup to be a conversation starter
- The design complements your wallpaper/lighting
- You prefer unique over generic

## Setup and Care Tips

1. **Clean your desk surface** before applying — dust affects grip
2. **Position the USB cable** to avoid snagging during intense gaming
3. **Start with lower brightness** — RGB can be overwhelming initially
4. **Clean with slightly damp cloth only** — no harsh chemicals
5. **Roll for storage** — folding can damage internal LED strips
6. **Avoid placing hot items** directly on the surface

## RGB Lighting Mode Guide

**Static modes:** Solid colors — red, blue, green, purple, etc.
**Breathing:** Slow fade in/out effect — great for ambient lighting
**Wave:** Color flows across the surface — dynamic but not distracting
**Rainbow:** Cycles through all colors — classic RGB effect
**Music reactive:** Some models sync with system audio (rare feature)
**Strobe/Flash:** Fast blinking — fun for parties, annoying for gaming

**Pro tip:** Most gamers settle on 2-3 favorite modes. Don't get too caught up in having 20+ options you'll never use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do RGB desk mats affect gaming performance?**
A: No impact on mouse tracking or keyboard input. Some mice perform better on cloth surfaces than hard plastic, so you might see improved precision.

**Q: How much power do RGB desk mats use?**
A: Very little — typically 5-10 watts max. About the same as charging a phone. Won't impact your electricity bill.

**Q: Can RGB desk mats damage wooden desks?**
A: Quality mats use non-marking rubber bases. Cheap ones might leave residue after months of use. Check reviews for rubber quality.

**Q: Do the LEDs get hot during use?**
A: Modern LED strips run cool. You might feel slight warmth where the power connector is, but the surface stays comfortable.

**Q: How long do RGB desk mats last?**
A: With normal use, 2-3 years minimum. LED strips are the most likely failure point, but quality brands often last 5+ years.

**Q: Can I wash RGB desk mats?**
A: Hand cleaning with damp cloth only. Never submerge in water or put in washing machine — you'll destroy the electronics.
